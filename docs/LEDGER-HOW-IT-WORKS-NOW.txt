PART 2. THE TWO IDEAS EVERYTHING RESTS ON
======================================================================

1. UNITS.
   The pool has one unit price = pool value / total units (the manager's units included).
   Money in buys units at the current price. Money out sells units at the current price.
   Trading changes the price, never anyone's units.
   So a deposit or a withdrawal can never look like profit or loss, as long as it is recorded.

2. EACH PERSON'S HIGH-WATER MARK (H, in USDT).
   Deposit D raises that person's H by D. The performance fee (20% for James) is charged only on
   the part of their value E (= units x price) that is above H.
   So each person pays only on the gain their own money made since it joined, and a loss is
   carried forward (they pay nothing until back above H).
   The manager's own units pay no fee.

PART 3. WHAT THE POOL VALUE (BOOK) IS, AND HOW IT MOVES TODAY
======================================================================

The ledger keeps a BOOK value (pool_equity rows) and, separately, a TRUE value read from the
bots' MEXC reports.

BOOK value
  - Rolls forward automatically from CLOSED trades only (auto_pool_mode = trades).
  - Each bot (BB-Core, I-Core, T-CORE) posts every closed trade about 5 minutes after the exit
    (closed_trades table, unique per wallet + MEXC position id). The amount is MEXC's realized
    result, which already includes trading fees and funding.
  - Each booking is a pool entry "Auto (closed_trade): ADA SHORT closed, realized -5.50 USDT".
  - Open trades are NEVER written into the book. They stay out until they close.
  - The bots cannot type or change the price any other way (bot_equity_sync = off).

TRUE value (used only for money joining or leaving)
  V     = sum of wallet equity (includes open P&L)
          - money recorded but not yet admitted
          - money redeemed but not yet sent
  check = V - (book + sum of open P&L); must be within max(0.50 USDT, 0.05%) or the flow is BLOCKED
  price = V / total units

Wallet reports must be: every wallet fresh, none "suspect" (a >50% jump needs manager acceptance),
all within 10 minutes of each other, and the expected number of wallets present. If not,
nothing is priced (never zero, never a guess).

Every true-value deposit / payout / settlement stores a FLOW CHECKPOINT: the exact numbers and
wallet reports it used (append-only audit).

Two guards that matter in practice:
  - A person-typed pool entry (old Update Pool total, old deposit, old payout) is REFUSED while
    flow_pricing = true_value. Only the true-value functions, automatic trade bookings and
    corrections may write to the pool.
  - Corrections to the book (book_corrections) are prepared, previewed, then applied in order by
    the manager. Two were applied Nov 7 (-4.9258 missed SOL trade, +0.8582 double count).

PART 4. SCENARIOS: MONEY IN
======================================================================

4.1  An investor pays pesos (P2P / GCash) and USDT arrives in MEXC   [LIVE]
     Steps (manager, Record > Money in):
       a. Record the deposit: pesos paid + USDT actually received. It waits (pending).
          The effective rate (pesos / USDT) is worked out, never typed.
       b. Money sits in Spot (not traded). Mark "seen in the exchange".
       c. Admit at TRUE value: units = USDT credited / true price.
       d. Move the money Spot -> Futures yourself in MEXC.
     Result: the joiner gets none of earlier profit or loss; nobody else's value changes.
     Their H = the credited USDT. The pool's price does not move.
     Several deposits can be admitted "as of" an earlier complete wallet reading, which is
     how the Nov 7 cash-in was priced (03:31:06 UTC reading, price 0.905664).

4.2  Peso top-up at the LIVE rate   [LIVE, owner rule Nov 7]
     The owner's rule: an investor's pesos count at the live PHP/USD reference rate, whatever
     MEXC actually delivered. If less USDT arrived than pesos / live rate, the fund manager
     (Raf) covers the gap from HIS OWN units. Nobody else pays.
     Example (done): James paid 5,000 pesos. Live rate 63.083992 -> credited 79.259410 USDT.
     Only 66.40 USDT arrived, so Raf topped up 12.859410 USDT = 14.07505444 units at 0.91363.
     One pool entry; pool value and total units unchanged. James's H rises by the top-up, so
     no fee is ever charged on it. Refused: rate >2% from stored reference, book pricing,
     manager short of units, non-investor.
     Raf's own peso deposits need no top-up (he is the manager).

4.3  Admission spread (1 peso per USDT, surplus stays the manager's)   [BUILT, not applied]
     Investor credited at pesos / (rate + 1); the small surplus becomes the manager's own units
     (two linked pending deposits admitted together) or, with "manager reallocation", credited
     units move from the manager's holding. Price and other investors unchanged.
     Open policy point: investors can infer the spread; telling them is the owner's call.

4.4  Top-up by an existing investor, trade open   [LIVE]
     Works now. True-value pricing does not need a flat account. Their H rises by the top-up.
     Their earlier unrealised gain stays theirs and is charged once, later.

4.5  Deposit waiting when a trade closes / waiting at settlement   [LIVE]
     Waiting money is subtracted from the exchange total, so it is never counted as profit.
     At a settlement, waiting deposits join AFTER the fees, at the settlement price.

4.6  Manager adds own capital   [LIVE]
     Record as a deposit for Raf. Buys units, no fee on his own money.

4.7  Investor pays the manager directly (cash), nothing goes to MEXC   [partly]
     Old "units from manager" function is REFUSED under true-value pricing. Use the peso
     top-up / reallocation path instead (admission spread's reallocation, once applied).

4.8  Deposit entered twice (double click / lost reply)   [LIVE]
     Every money step carries a request id. A repeat returns the stored answer, not a second deposit.

4.9  Deposit handed back / typed wrong   [LIVE]
     Waiting deposit can be handed back; nothing in the books changes (6-second Undo in the app).

4.10 Money sent to MEXC and NOT recorded   [NO, caught not prevented]
     A forgotten deposit looks like trading profit. True-value checks will usually block
     (gap vs book + open P&L), and the manager must record it. The bots do not yet send MEXC's
     deposit/withdrawal history.

4.11 Deposit in pesos, how it is shown   [LIVE]
     Owner rule (Nov 8): a deposit is shown as the pesos put in (+5,000 pesos) with
     "= 79.0139 USDT" underneath when known. "Put in" is one green peso card; "Withdrawn" is a red
     card only for real payouts. The Raf-to-James top-up is NOT shown as Raf's withdrawal.
     Facts recorded: Raf put in 21,153 pesos in total; James 5,000.

PART 5. SCENARIOS: MONEY OUT
======================================================================

5.1  Investor requests a withdrawal   [LIVE]
     Typed in USDT. Units = USDT / unit price. Flow: request > approve > quote > redeem >
     send from MEXC > mark "sent" > completed. Each step runs once only.
     The preview is the database's own quote (the real withdrawal, rolled back).

5.2  Partial withdrawal in profit   [LIVE]
     Units sold at the TRUE price. Fee = rate x s x (E - H), where s = share withdrawn.
     H becomes H x (1 - s). The remaining units keep their share of the uncharged gain.

5.3  Partial withdrawal at or below H   [LIVE]
     No fee. H falls by s x E; the WHOLE shortfall stays with the remaining units, so
     withdrawing can't be used to escape a loss.

5.4  Full exit in profit / full exit at a loss   [LIVE]
     In profit: fee on the whole gain, H = 0. In a loss: the shortfall is remembered; if they
     return, H = old shortfall + new deposit.

5.5  Withdrawal while a trade is open   [LIVE]
     Priced at true value (open P&L included), no need to close anything.

5.6  More requested than can leave MEXC (margin tied up)   [BUILT, payout_cap_mode off]
     Cap = smallest of equity / withdrawable / available per wallet, less a 5% + 5 USDT buffer.
     When on: "PAYOUT QUEUED", nothing saved. Today the ledger does not know what is
     withdrawable; check MEXC yourself. Investors already see "can leave now" vs "locked in
     trades" (my_true_value).

5.7  Minimum withdrawal 500 pesos   [LIVE]
     Checked in USDT at the stored fx reference rate (<=7 days old, stored whenever the manager
     opens the app), never at a cash-in rate. Taking everything out is always allowed.
     If no fresh reference exists the minimum is NOT checked (a small request goes through).

5.8  Lock-up (first deposit 3 months)   [LIVE as guidance]
     lock_enforced = 0: manager can approve with a written reason (logged).

5.9  Manager pays the investor from his own pocket (MEXC untouched)   [partly]
     Old function refused under true-value; use reallocation once admission-spread is applied.

5.10 Last holder takes everything, total has >6 decimals   [NO, refused safely]
     "payout exceeds pool equity"; nothing saved. Workaround: 6-decimal total.

5.11 Payout failed / retried   [LIVE]
     failed > retry > sent > completed; each step once.

5.12 Money left MEXC and NOT recorded   [NO, caught not prevented]
     Looks like a trading loss shared by everyone. Record the withdrawal first, then send.

PART 6. SCENARIOS: PROFIT SHARING (THE 2-MONTH SETTLEMENT)
======================================================================

Current engine: investor_hwm_v2, fee_timing = immediate. 0 settlements have been made so far.
Nov-Dec period ends Dec 31; its window is Jan 1-7 (Manila). The Sep-Oct period (window Nov 1-7)
was not settled, and the next settlement covers everything since the last one.

6.1  Normal settlement in profit
     fee = rate x max(0, E - H); H := max(H, E - fee).
     Fee moves as units from the investor to the manager (fee_out / fee_in); total units,
     value and price are unchanged. Fee units are rounded DOWN at the 8th decimal.

6.2  Period ends in a loss
     No fee, H stays; nothing charged until back above H.

6.3  Gain that reverses right after being charged
     With "immediate" the fee is NOT refunded. "confirmed" mode (fee only on gain still there
     at the NEXT settlement) is [BUILT, off]; it needs updated investor terms first.

6.4  Settlement while trades are open
     Under "immediate" it needs a written "open_positions" exception. With the true-value
     settlement (admin_close_quarter_tv) the wallet reports are the exchange evidence.

6.5  Someone joined during the period     Pays only on their own gain since joining.
6.6  Someone withdrew during the period   Their share was settled at withdrawal; the rest now.
6.7  Fee rate raised   Starts after the next settlement. Lowered: applies at once. 0%: no fee,
     and gains made at 0% are never charged later.
6.8  Late (after 7 days) or backdated (>60 min)   Refused unless a written exception (>=20
     characters) is approved; each exception is used once, audited.
6.9  Settled twice / double click   Refused / replayed.
6.10 Reconciliation   Ledger must agree with itself; every exchange transfer matched;
     otherwise refused and NO exception can override.
6.11 Manager's own money   Not charged. Fee basis = external_investors; manager capital and
     fee entitlement are tracked apart.
6.12 Stale preview   settlement_preview_mode would refuse a settlement whose preview is out of
     date [BUILT, off until the new website is published].

PART 9. SAFETY NETS (always on)
======================================================================

  - One money write at a time (advisory lock); all writes are single transactions.
  - Request-id (idempotency) on every money step: retries never repeat.
  - Books check after every write: units sum = pool total; equity = price x units; no negative
    units; fee units net to zero. Any failure rolls back everything.
  - Only the NEWEST entry can be undone; a correction can never be undone; settlements cannot
    be undone. Older mistakes need a new, explained correcting entry.
  - Backups export every ledger table (admin_backup_coverage proves nothing is missing); never
    passwords or keys.
  - Wallet reports are append-only, replay-protected, one secret per wallet, purged after 30 days.
  - Closed trades: one row per wallet + MEXC position id; a trade can be booked once.
  - Anything with a moved price over 25% asks for confirmation; a wallet jump over 50% is
    held as suspect until accepted with a reason.
