BOT DASHBOARD - COMPLETE INVENTORY OF THE CURRENT PAGE (before the revamp)
Written Nov 8, 2026 from the source: bot-dashboard/index.html, app.js, research.js, lib/perf.js, lib/aggregate.js, api/data.js.

==========================================================================================
0. HOW THE PAGE WORKS (applies to every tab)
==========================================================================================
Files
  bot-dashboard/index.html        page shell, 7 tabs, login panel, trade dialog
  bot-dashboard/app.js            all rendering (1105 lines)
  bot-dashboard/research.js       pure stat helpers for the Research tab (BotResearch)
  bot-dashboard/style.css         look
  bot-dashboard/regime-history.json   past market regimes (Research tab)
  api/data.js                     GET /api/data (password cookie, ETag / 304, 10 s response cache, VERSION 'perf-7')
  lib/aggregate.js, perf.js, store.js, ingest.js, schema.js, commands.js   backend

Data feed (one JSON, /api/data), built by aggregate():
  status, open, trades, equity, per_coin, overall, skipped, compare (all BB-Core, in the chosen Data mode)
  bots.bbcore | bots.icore | bots.tcore      short view of each LIVE bot (T-CORE only if it has reported)
  total_capital                              the three live bots' balances added together
  labs.bb1h | labs.bbtr                      paper lab bots (BB-1h lab, T-CORE lab)
  news_labs.bbn | icn | tcn                  news lab paper bots (BB-CORE / I-CORE / T-CORE + news gate); each also has news_skips { total, recent }
  recorder                                   data recorder heartbeat (regime, market, slippage, health)
  wallet_capital, wallet_history             real MEXC balances per account, every 5 minutes
  capital, capital_flows                     BB-Core balance, deposits/payouts
  uptime, activity                           BB-Core heartbeat gaps, newest 80 log lines
  perf.bbcore | icore | bb1h | bbtr | tcore | bbn | icn | tcn  per-bot analysis (lib/perf.js)

Bots on the page
  BB-Core      live   4h, key "bbcore"   (the main bot; its data is the "default" page data)
  I-Core       live   key "icore"
  T-CORE       live   key "tcore", own MEXC wallet "tcore"
  BB-1h lab    paper  key "bb1h"   (lab: 1h BB-Core, five coins)
  T-CORE lab   paper  key "bbtr"   (lab: same strategy as T-CORE, paper)
  BB-CORE news paper key "bbn"   (news lab: BB-Core 4h + news gate; event_id prefix bbn|; no Telegram)
  I-CORE news  paper key "icn"   (news lab: I-Core + news gate; prefix icn|)
  T-CORE news  paper key "tcn"   (news lab: T-CORE + news gate; prefix tcn|; compared with bbtr)
  News Lab tab (Research Lab > News Lab): backtest (news-lab-backtest.json), shadow verdicts on the real bots trades
               (news-gate.js + cryptobai.vercel.app/api/gate-inputs), news labs vs their counterparts
  Recorder     data only, no trades

Controls shared by the whole page
  Header: title, last-update time / alive dot, menu button
  Menu:   Data mode (Paper / Live), Times (UTC / Local), Theme (auto / light / dark), Refresh, Log out
  Login panel (password) and a boot spinner on first load
  Tab bar (#mainTabs): Overview, Performance, Calendar, Research, Verify, Log, Status
  Bot selector (bot): BB-Core / I-Core / T-CORE / BB-1h lab / T-CORE lab. It decides which bot the
     Performance, Calendar, Research and Log tabs show. It is NOT used by Overview or Status.
  Range selector (bot.range) for Overview chart and tables
  Chart mode (bot.chart), period (bot.period), calendar metric (bot.calmetric)
  Trade dialog (#tradeDlg): opens when you click a trade row; full trade card
  Remembered in the browser (localStorage): bot.tz, bot.mode, bot.range, bot.chart, bot.period,
     bot.view, bot.tab, bot.lastCapital, bot.calmetric, bot.theme
  Each tab renders only when opened (RENDER map), then re-renders on refresh.

Strategy descriptions held in app.js (STRATS): BB-Core, T-CORE, I-Core. trailHow() holds the T-CORE
trailing-stop explanation text.

Pass/fail rules (lib/perf.js PROFILES, "stop-and-review"): BB-Core, I-Core, BB-1h lab, T-CORE lab and
T-CORE (T-CORE uses the T-CORE lab's numbers). BB-Core and I-Core have their own expectations; the
labs and T-CORE judge themselves against their own backtest (win 21%, average +1.286R, 7.7 trades a
month, worst drop 29.7R, longest losing streak 26; warn 30R, halt 45R; judged after 100 trades).
BB-1h lab: avg +0.457R, win 35.2%, 19.8 a month, worst drop 31.2R, streak 21, warn 26R, halt 39R, after 150 trades.

==========================================================================================
1. OVERVIEW tab   (panels pKpis, pChecks, pBots, pChart, pOpen, pTrades)
==========================================================================================
1.1 KPI row (pKpis)
    - total capital (all live bots added together, from total_capital / wallets) with its change and %,
      "paper" tag where a part is a paper balance, "missing" note when a bot has no balance yet
    - change since start (capital that was added or removed is NOT counted), time-weighted return %
    - closed trades, win rate, total R, profit factor, max drawdown (R) of the bot shown
    - open positions count, mode, last update
1.2 Check bar (pChecks): one-line verdicts of the stop-and-review checks per bot (verdicts(d)).
    Today it covers BB-Core and I-Core only. T-CORE is not in it.
1.3 Bot cards (pBots): renderBots shows [BB-Core, I-Core] only. Per card: label, mode, alive/stale,
    balance, closed trades, win rate, total R, open count, kill switch, drawdown vs halt.
    T-CORE live has NO card. Labs have no card.
1.4 Chart (pChart): cumulative performance / balance chart. Modes: wallet balances of all accounts added
    together over time (wallet_history), BB-Core balance, performance without capital movements, cumulative R
    and drawdown. Capital steps (deposits / payouts) drawn as labelled markers.
1.5 Open positions (pOpen): BB-Core and I-Core only: coin, side, entry, stop, target, current price, current R,
    price note ("last closed 4h candle"). T-CORE open trades (with trailing stop, locked R, best R, trail moves)
    are not listed here.
1.6 Latest trades (pTrades): newest closed trades of the selected view, click opens the trade dialog.

==========================================================================================
2. PERFORMANCE tab   (pPerf, pCoins, pPeriods, pBreak, pExec, pRolling)   works for ANY bot in the selector
==========================================================================================
2.1 pPerf   headline numbers: trades, wins, win rate, profit factor, average R, total R, max drawdown R,
            longest losing streak, versus the bot's expectation (backtest) with a pass/watch/stop mark
2.2 pCoins  per coin table: trades, win rate, profit factor, average R, max drawdown, longest losing streak
2.3 pPeriods results by day / month / year (R and trades)
2.4 pBreak  breakdown by side (long/short) and by exit reason (stop, target, trail, time ...)
2.5 pExec   execution quality: slippage (R and %), delay, fees (R), costs kind, versus expected
2.6 pRolling rolling window result (BB-Core / I-Core rules: roll_n, roll_stop_r)
            Also: the checks list (drawdown warn/stop, rolling, target, streak, slippage, compare, edge)
            and the verdict.
Trend capture for T-CORE and T-CORE lab (trend block): trades with MFE, reached 1R / 3R / 5R / 10R,
            median MFE, median MAE, median give-back, median bars, trail moves, funding R, slippage bp, vs the
            backtest's own rates (only expectation set for "bbtr": 55% reach 1R, 31% 3R, 22% 5R, 9% 10R,
            median MFE 1.33, MAE -1.09, give-back 2.23, 32 bars).

==========================================================================================
3. CALENDAR tab   (pCal, pCalDay, pCalYear)   any bot
==========================================================================================
3.1 pCal      month grid, each day coloured by R (or trades / dollars via bot.calmetric), month total
3.2 pCalDay   click a day: that day's trades
3.3 pCalYear  year overview: months with data, month totals, year total

==========================================================================================
4. RESEARCH tab   (pRHow, pRRegime, pRSlip, pRRec, pRReady, pREdge, pRDist, pRSplit, pRTrades)   any bot
==========================================================================================
4.1 pRHow    how this strategy trades (STRATS text, T-CORE trailHow text)
4.2 pRRegime market regime of each coin and of the market now (recorder.regime, market, market_state) and
             regime history (regime-history.json)
4.3 pRSlip   measured slippage per coin (recorder.slippage)
4.4 pRRec    recorder health: alive/stale (30 min), what it records
4.5 pRReady  live-readiness checklist: sample >= 25 trades, timing <= 15 min, costs <= 0.07R,
             compare match, drawdown under the halt
4.6 pREdge   edge with a 95% confidence interval (average R)
4.7 pRDist   histogram of R (edges -2, -1, -0.5, 0, 0.5, 1, 2, 3)
4.8 pRSplit  splits: by weekday, by hour block (00-06, 06-12, 12-18, 18-24), by holding time
             (<4h, 4-12h, 12-24h, 1-3 days, 3+ days), by coin, by side
4.9 pRTrades per-trade research cards: signal candle, intended price vs fill, slippage R, delay, fees,
             near-threshold %, size, qty, risk $, balance used/after, funding

==========================================================================================
5. VERIFY tab   (pCheckList, pCompare, pCheckList2, pCompare2)
==========================================================================================
5.1 pCheckList / pCheckList2  stop-and-review checks (both lists exist; the second repeats for I-Core)
5.2 pCompare / pCompare2      live-vs-backtest comparison result: match / mismatches, matched count,
                              live-only, backtest-only, refused orders (explained mismatches), last 5 runs
Only BB-Core and I-Core are covered (no compare/check entry for T-CORE live). Panels are duplicated.

==========================================================================================
6. LOG tab   (pActivity, pSkipped)
==========================================================================================
6.1 pActivity  newest 80 events, newest first: entry, exit (reason, R, $), skip, candle check
               (total R, balance), compare, capital flow
6.2 pSkipped   skipped signals (newest 200) with reason and detail, plus counts by reason

==========================================================================================
7. STATUS tab   (pNow, pSystem, pServer)
==========================================================================================
7.1 pNow     "now" strip: alive/stale, last heartbeat, next wake, mode, kill switch, drawdown vs halt,
             enabled coins, last candle per coin (BB-Core)
7.2 pSystem  version, commit, uptime, 7-day uptime % with downtime list (gaps over 4h20m) and restarts
7.3 pServer  VPS status loaded inline (loadVpsInline, api/vps.js): services, disk/memory, commands (ask bot now)
Bot health is BB-Core centred; I-Core, T-CORE and the labs are not shown with the same detail.
