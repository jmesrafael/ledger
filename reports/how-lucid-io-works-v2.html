About the numbers on this page. The 2026 results and the since-2020 results are a backtest: the Lucid'io trading rules replayed on actual historical market prices of the coins traded, including trading fees, slippage and funding costs, at 1% risk per trade. It is a simulation of real market data, not real accounts and not real people. Real results will differ, and can be worse.

Only put in money you can afford to lose

The market is erratic. Nobody, including us, can guarantee it will work every time. The strategy is based on statistics and probability: it aims to win more than it loses over many trades, but any single week, month or even year can go wrong, and the market may behave differently from the past.

1 · The pool

One account, everyone owns a slice

All the money sits together in one MEXC trading account. Instead of tracking pesos per person, the ledger gives each person units. If you own 20% of the units, you own 20% of whatever the account is worth, today and every day.

An example pool, not real people. When the trading makes or loses money, every slice grows or shrinks by the same percentage. Nobody's slice changes size because someone else adds or takes out money.

2 · Your money's journey

What happens to your ₱1,000

The labels in green are what you will see next to your money in the app.

1

You send the money Waiting

You hand the money to the fund manager. It is written down straight away, but it is not in the pool yet: it gains nothing and loses nothing.

2

It is seen arriving in MEXC Waiting to join

Money only counts once it has actually reached the trading account. Until then it stays outside the pool, so it can never be counted as profit by mistake.

3

It joins at a checked price In the pool

The fund manager checks the account balance, confirms it, and only then lets your money join. It buys units at that day's price, so you never share in profit made before you joined, and never pay for losses from before either.

4

Your units ride the trading Estimate

Your units stay the same; their price moves with the trading. Your balance in the app is an estimate until a fee settlement or a withdrawal fixes the final numbers.

5

Every two months, the fee is settled Final

If your value is above your best level so far, 10% of the new profit is charged. If not, nothing is charged. More on this in section 4.

6

You take money out when you need it Paid

Ask in the app. It is paid within 7 business days, at the value on the day it is paid out.

4 · The fee

10% of new profit, never twice

Each investor has a best level (the "high-water mark"). It starts at what you put in, and rises with every deposit. Every two months:

Above your best level

10% of the part above it is charged. Your best level moves up to your new value.

Below your best level

No fee. A loss has to be made back first, before any fee is due again.

Money in or out

Deposits are never profit and withdrawals are never losses. They do not move the fee.

5 · 2026, five friends (backtest)

Different amounts, different months: what each one made

Five example friends join the same pool in 2026, each with a different amount and in a different month. Every one of them gets the same trading result per unit from the day they join; what differs is when they joined and how much. The fee is 10% of new profit above each person's best level, settled at the end of February, April, June and August. Results are up to Oct 21, 2026, shown in pesos at ₱58 per USDT.

Note: why some friends are negative, and why that is normal

Look at the table again: Dana and Eli are below what they put in, and Cris is about even. That is not because they did anything wrong. They are simply still early: they joined only a few weeks or months ago, and that short stretch happened to include a dip in the market.

  • Short term, anything can happen. Over a few weeks the market can go down as easily as up. Someone who joins right before a dip starts in the red.
  • Long term is where the strategy works. It is built to grow slowly over many months, with small wins adding up and losses kept small. It needs time to play out.
  • The friends who joined earliest are the ones ahead. Ana (January) and Ben (March) had time to ride out the dips, and are up after fees.

So please think of this as long-term money: plan to leave it for at least a year, and do not judge it by the first few weeks. In the backtest, every full year since 2020 ended higher than it started (see section 9):

6 · In the app

What the numbers mean

Estimated value after fees is your main number. It is marked Estimate because the trading keeps moving it.

If the latest balance has not been checked yet, the app says Waiting for verification and shows your last checked value instead of a number that might be wrong.

Profit-sharing records are marked Final: those fees are settled and will not change.

Money you put in and money you took out are always shown on their own lines, never mixed into profit or loss.

7 · Taking money out

Ask any time; paid within 7 business days

Tap Request withdrawal on Home. Add where you want to be paid (GCash or bank) first. You can take part of it or all of it. The minimum is ₱500, and taking everything is always allowed.

Why a request can wait

Up to two trades can be open at once. A payout is valued only when both trades are closed and the balance is checked, so nobody is paid out at a half-finished trade's price. The app tells you the reason while you wait.

The 3-month lock-up

Your first deposit has a 3-month lock-up as a guideline: please plan to leave it in that long. If you need money earlier, ask; the fund manager decides, and any exception is written down.

Withdrawal fee: none for now. A small one may be introduced later, lower the longer your account has been open. You will be told before it starts.
8 · Risks

Please read this part twice

Please remember what could happen

  • Never use money you need for rent, food, school, bills, emergencies, or money you borrowed. Only money you could lose without it hurting your life.
  • No guarantee. We work with statistics and probability, not certainty. Good past results do not mean the next trade, month or year will be good.
  • The market can change. Prices can move faster and further than ever before, or behave in ways the past data never showed. The strategy may then lose more than expected.
  • Losses can be large. You can lose a big part, or in the worst case all, of what you put in.
  • Be ready for red periods. There will be weeks and months where your balance is down. That is part of it; please do not put in money that would make you panic when that happens.

!You can lose money

Crypto moves fast. The pool can fall sharply and you can lose a large part or all of what you put in. Only use money you can afford to lose.

!Past results are not a promise

The numbers below come from a backtest: the strategy replayed on old prices. The coins were picked with hindsight. Real results will differ.

!The exchange and trading errors

The money sits on MEXC. An exchange problem, a hack or a trading error could cause losses that the strategy did not plan for.

!Not a bank, not insured

This is a private record of a share in a pooled account among friends and family. It is not a bank deposit, not insured and not a registered investment product.
