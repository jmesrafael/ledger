1 · Your slice

The pool is one big pizza

All the money sits together in one trading account at an exchange. Instead of tracking everyone's pesos, the ledger cuts the pool into equal slices. We call a slice a unit. You own some slices, other investors own some, and so does the fund manager.

An example pizza with made-up people. Tap the buttons to see what happens. The rule is always the same: the pizza gets bigger or smaller, and every slice grows or shrinks by the same percent. Nobody's slices change because someone else joins or leaves.

2 · How you join

From your pocket to a bigger pool, in 6 steps

Think of a group savings pot that is traded by one person for everyone. The coloured labels are what you will see next to your money in the app.

1

You send your money Waiting

You hand the money to the fund manager. It is written down straight away, but it is not working yet: it can't gain and it can't lose.

2

We check that it arrived Waiting to join

Your money only counts once it has really reached the trading account at the exchange. Until then it stays outside the pool, so it can never be counted as profit by mistake.

3

You get your slices In the pool

Your money joins at that day's price and you receive units (the slices of the pizza in section 1). You never share in gains made before you joined, and you never pay for losses from before either.

4

The pool is traded Estimate

The fund manager trades the pool for everyone. You keep the same number of slices; what each slice is worth goes up and down. The balance you see in the app is an estimate until a settlement or a withdrawal makes it final.

5

Every two months, we settle Final

If your money is worth more than ever before, 20% of the new profit is the fee. If not, there is no fee. How the fee works.

6

You take your money out when you need it Paid

Ask the fund manager. You are paid at the value on the day you are paid.

3 · How the pool trades

Lose small, win big

The pool is traded by automated systems that follow fixed rules and watch the markets day and night. Before a trade opens, it already has a stop: the price where it closes if the market goes the wrong way. What a trade can lose at its stop is called 1R. Losses are cut at about 1R. Wins are not capped: when a trend runs, the stop follows the price (a trailing stop), and a trade can make many times what it risked.

~1 in 3trades win, in the tests on 2020–2026 prices. Most trades lose a little.
1Rabout the most a trade loses. 1R = 2.5% of the pool's balance at the time.
10R+what the best trades made. A few big wins pay for many small losses.
14–17losses in a row: the longest runs in those tests. Expect them, they are normal.

6 · The fee

20% of new profit, never charged twice

There is only one fee, and you only pay it when you make new profit. Every investor has a best level: the highest your money has ever been worth. It starts at what you put in and rises with every deposit. Every two months we look at where you are:

Above your best level

20% of the part above it is the fee. Your best level then moves up to your new value.

Below your best level

No fee. A loss has to be made back first, before any fee is due again.

Money in or out

Deposits are never counted as profit and withdrawals are never counted as losses. They don't change the fee.

Why a request can wait a little

Several trades can be open at once. A payout is worked out only when both trades are closed and the balance is checked, so nobody is paid at the price of a half-finished trade. The app tells you the reason while you wait.

The 3-month lock-up

Your first deposit has a 3-month lock-up as a guideline: please plan to leave it in that long. If you need money earlier, ask; the fund manager decides, and any exception is written down.

Withdrawal fee: none for now. A small one may be introduced later, lower the longer your account has been open. You will be told before it starts.
8 · Risks

Please read this part twice

Please remember what could happen

  • Never use money you need for rent, food, school, bills, emergencies, or money you borrowed. Only money you could lose without it hurting your life.
  • No guarantee. We work with statistics and probability, not certainty. Good past results do not mean the next trade, month or year will be good.
  • The market can change. Prices can move faster and further than ever before, or behave in ways the past never showed. The plan may then lose more than expected.
  • Losses can be large. You can lose a big part, or in the worst case all, of what you put in.
  • Be ready for red periods. There will be weeks and months where your balance is down. That is part of it; please don't put in money that would make you panic when that happens.

What "2.5% risk per trade" really means

Before every trade, the pool puts 2.5% of its current balance at risk (about ₱125 on ₱5,000). Wins make the next trade bigger, losses make it smaller, and the pool can have many trades open at once, one per market. That is why results swing.

Source: tests on past prices up to . A lower risk setting is calmer; 2.5% is what the pool uses, so expect red months and the occasional deep red stretch.

You can lose money

The market moves fast. The pool can fall sharply and you can lose a large part or all of what you put in. Only use money you can afford to lose.

Past results are not a promise

The numbers on this page come from a test: the trading plan replayed on old prices. The markets were chosen after seeing how they did. Real results will differ.

The exchange and trading errors

The money sits at an exchange. An exchange problem, a hack or a trading error could cause losses that the plan did not expect.

Not a bank, not insured

This is a private record of a share in a pooled account among friends and family. It is not a bank deposit, not insured and not a registered investment product.

9 · Questions

What people usually ask

What exactly do I own?

Slices of the pool, called units. Everyone's slices are worth the same, so when the pool grows or shrinks, your money grows or shrinks by the same percent as everyone else's. See the pizza.

When does my money start working?

Only once it has really reached the trading account at the exchange. Until then it is written down but stays outside the pool: it can't gain and it can't lose. Then it joins at that day's price. See how you join.

Is the live record real?

Yes. The pool has traded real money since Oct 24, 2026. The live section reads the pool's wallets every 5 minutes, and the losing days are shown too. The year-by-year tables further down are a test on past prices, and they say so.

Why do so many trades lose?

That is how the rules are built: each loss is cut small, at about 1R, and the wins are allowed to run. In the tests about 1 trade in 3 won, and runs of 14 to 17 losses in a row happened. A few big wins paid for them. See lose small, win big.

What does it cost?

Only one fee: 20% of new profit, above the highest your money has ever been worth. It is worked out every two months. After a loss there is no fee until the loss is made back. There is no withdrawal fee for now; if a small one is ever added, you are told before it starts. See the fee.

Can I take my money out any time?

Just ask the fund manager; you are paid at the value on the day you are paid. The minimum is ₱500, and taking everything is always allowed. Your first deposit has a 3-month lock-up as a guideline; if you need money earlier, ask. See taking money out.

How am I paid?

To a bank account, GCash, Maya, GoTyme or another e-wallet, with a QR code if you have one (GCash, InstaPay or QR Ph). You add it in the app before your first request.

Why does the app say "estimate"?

Between settlements your balance moves with the open trades. It becomes final at a settlement (every two months) or when you are paid out.

Can I lose money?

Yes. You can lose a large part, or in the worst case all, of what you put in. There will be red weeks and months. Please read the risks twice before you decide.

Is this a bank or a registered investment?

No. It is a private record of a share in a pooled trading account among friends and family. It is not a bank deposit, it is not insured, and it is not a registered investment product.

10 · Is this for you?

Decide calmly

It may suit you if

  • the money can stay in for at least a year
  • you can watch a red month without panic
  • you want a share of a traded pool instead of trading yourself
  • you have read the risks and the fee
  • you have an emergency fund set aside, separate from this money
  • you accept that a loss is possible

It is not for you if

  • you need the money for rent, food, school, bills or emergencies
  • the money is borrowed
  • you need a fixed or guaranteed return
  • losing it would hurt your life
  • you expect quick or easy profit
  • you are only joining because others did

Reality check before you commit

Be honest with each one. If any answer is "no", wait.

  • If this money dropped by half, I would still be okay.
  • I do not need it back in the next 12 months.
  • It is not borrowed, and not the money for my bills, school or emergencies.
  • I understand a losing month, or even a losing year, can happen and I will not pull out in a panic.
  • I know past tests are not a promise, and nothing here is guaranteed.
  • I know the fee is 20% of new profit, and I have read the risks.
  • I am deciding for myself, not because someone else joined.
