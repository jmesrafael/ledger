How to read the numbers. R is one unit of risk: what a trade loses when it hits its stop. At $5 risk a trade, +100R is +$500. Worst drop is the deepest fall from a peak, counted trade by trade. Losing streak is the most losing trades in a row. Losing months is the share of calendar months that ended down.

How it was tested

  • Data: Binance spot 4-hour candles, every candle from January 1, 2020 to November 11, 2026. Each coin counts from when it has 200 candles of history.
  • Rules: the same engine that was checked trade by trade against the bots' own code. One position per coin. A signal is read on a closed candle and filled at the next candle's open.
  • Costs: 0.05% fee per side, 0.02% slippage per fill, real funding payments. Everything was also rerun with fees and slippage doubled.
  • Search: about 170 single changes across the three bots first, then 1,947 combinations of the promising ones. A version only counts if it beats today in all four columns, in both halves of the history, and on most coins.

Is the test honest about the future?

Two things can make a backtest look better than real trading: rules that secretly use later prices, and settings that were tuned on the same history they are scored on. Both were tested.

1. No future sight and no repaint: passed

The history was cut at 528 random dates. Each time the bot was rerun knowing only the candles up to the cut, exactly as it would live. Every signal on every candle, and every trade closed before the cut, was then compared with the full run.

Zero differences. A signal never changes after its candle closes, and no trade depends on a candle that comes later. Inside a candle the test assumes the worst: if the stop and a better price are both touched, the stop is counted first, and a stop hit through a gap fills at the worse open price.

2. Settings chosen without seeing the future: the gains shrink but hold

Before each year from 2023 to 2026, the best setting was picked using only the years before it, then used to trade that year. The four unseen years were joined together and compared with today's rules on the same years.

What was done

All of this went live on the VPS on Nov 14, 2026 at about 01:25 UTC. The first candle judged by the new rules closes at 04:00 UTC; trades opened before it keep the old rules until they close.

  1. T-CORE rule change: done. Profit +1081R → +1191R, worst drop 50R → 33R, losing streak 27 → 14, and its one losing year (2023) turns positive. It is the surest of the three.
  2. I-CORE rule change and ETH: done. +697R → +822R with the drop down from 33R to 25R. Do not count on the shorter losing streak. ETH was added on top (see the long-history check).
  3. BB-CORE rule change: done, for the profit. +663R → +742R. Its smaller drop did not hold on unseen years. The time exit moved from 48 to 42 candles, which reopened a setting locked on Nov 2.
  4. BNB on T-CORE: dropped. It looked good over 2020–2026, but its whole history shows one trade making 67% of its profit. It never went live. No coin was removed from any bot.

What not to do

  • Fixed profit targets (2R, 4R, 8R and so on) cut profit on every bot. These bots earn from a few very large trades, and a target cuts exactly those.
  • Taking half off early and breakeven after +1R also cut profit: trades get closed before they run.
  • Filters (ADX, volume, BTC trend) and longs only remove more profit than loss.
