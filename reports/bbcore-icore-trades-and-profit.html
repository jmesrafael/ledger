The short answer

  • BB-Core trades about twice as often. 18.4 trades a month against 9.3 for I-Core (about 221 against 112 a year). Since 2021 the steady rate is about 20 and 10.
  • BB-Core is the steadier earner. It makes money in 74% of months and its worst month is −8.1%. I-Core makes money in only 52% of months, but its winners are huge (best month +68%).
  • Every calendar year was positive for both bots with a fresh ₱10,000 every January at 2.5% risk. Year-end balances ran from ₱20,845 to ₱66,557 for BB-Core and from ₱13,284 to ₱61,352 for I-Core.
  • Treat the numbers as an upper bound. It is a test on past prices. The coins and settings were chosen with hindsight, and the first small trades on a ₱10,000 account may not be fillable (contract sizes).

7. How these numbers were made, and what they leave out

Method

  • Trades: the real backtest code of each bot (lucidio-core.js for BB-Core, icore-core.js for I-Core), unchanged, on the stored 4-hour Binance candles for SOL, ZEC, NEAR, ADA and GALA. Exchange fees, slippage and funding are already in each trade's result (R).
  • Money: each trade's R is multiplied by the amount risked when it opened: the smaller of the risk % times the balance at that moment, and the $10 cap. All five coins share one balance. Trades are processed in time order, and a trade only changes the balance once it closes.
  • Halt rule: no new trade once the account is 39R (BB-Core) or 35R (I-Core) below its best point. It never triggered here.
  • Section 3 uses each bot's bot-config.json (start $100, 0.5% risk, $10 cap) in one continuous account. Sections 4 to 6 use the live 2.5% risk and ₱10,000 (about $159), reset every 1 January, at ₱63 to the dollar. A year's account only includes trades that opened and closed within that year.

What it leaves out

  • Contract sizes. MEXC sells whole contracts. At ₱10,000 the risk per trade is only about $4, so some trades could not be opened or would be sized off-target. Early months would be lumpier and probably lower.
  • Hindsight. The coins and rule settings were chosen on this same history. Treat the figures as an upper bound, not a forecast.
  • Free margin and late entries. The live bot skips trades when margin is short or the entry is more than 15 minutes late. Not modelled.
  • Two bots, two accounts. The bots are shown separately. Running both on one account would share the same balance.
  • No performance fee. All figures are before the 20% fee in the ledger.
  • One fixed exchange rate (₱63 to the dollar) for every year.
This is a test on past prices, not a promise. Real results will differ, and you can lose a large part or all of the money you put in. Only use money you can afford to lose.
