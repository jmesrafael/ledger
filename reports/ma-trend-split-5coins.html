How the pesos work. The live bot's tiered risk (2.5% of the balance below $500, 2% below $1,000, 1.5% above, in pesos at ₱63). In the "capped" tables the total risk on one trade never exceeds $50 (₱3,150); each of n strategies is capped at its share, so splitting gets no extra risk. 5. How this was made, and the limits

  • Trades. bb-core and I-Core: the bots' own engines (js/bbcore-core.js, js/icore-core.js). MA Trend: js/matrend-core.js, which reproduces the chart-replay strategy "★ MA Trend · 4h ATR trail" (research/indicator-top10/replay-check.js: 2,254 trades, 0 mismatches). Signals use closed candles only and fill at the next open, so nothing repaints.
  • MA Trend rules. Close crosses above the 200 SMA (and the 200 SMA filter is satisfied); stop 2 ATR from the signal close; after every surviving candle the stop trails 14 ATR behind the best price; no profit target; safety exit after 600 candles. Long only. Chosen from 10 moving averages tested on BTC, ETH, SOL and ADA (research/indicator-top10/REPORT.md); here it is run on the bots' five coins.
  • Costs. I-Core model for the MA and I-Core (0.05% fee + 0.02% slippage each way, 0.005% funding per 4h held); bb-core with its default fee and slippage, without its funding files. Real funding would lower bb-core a little.
  • Risk. The live bot's tiers at ₱63/$ (2.5% below ₱31,500, 2% below ₱63,000, 1.5% above), applied to each strategy's own balance; cap ₱3,150 in total per trade slot where stated.
  • Split. n equal parts that start at the same balance, each running one strategy as its own account; never rebalanced; the balance is the sum of the parts. Closed trades only, booked on the day they close.

Limits

  • The three strategies trade the same 5 coins, so they are not independent; the MA is long-only, a bet that these coins keep drifting up.
  • Large peso numbers are compounding arithmetic on past trades, not forecasts, and no market would fill those sizes (see the capped tables).
  • The MA depends on a few big trends (2021, 2024, 2025) and loses in 2022-2023; it is the lumpiest of the three.
  • Not included: the Lucid'io fee, taxes, real fills on thin books, deposits and withdrawals.
