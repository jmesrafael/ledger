The short answer

  • 1st: BB-Core with no target. +0.45R per trade against +0.33R with the fixed target, the smallest drop of the three (23R), and better than the fixed target on all seven coins it was not designed on.
  • 2nd: I-Core. The highest average per trade (+0.87R) but 74% of its profit comes from its ten best trades, and it is the weakest of the three once those are taken out.
  • 3rd: BB-Core with the fixed target. The steadiest per trade (its ten best trades are only 8% of its profit) but the lowest average, and it caps every win at the target.
  • Best pair: BB-Core with no target + I-Core. The two almost never win in the same months (correlation 0.09). It earns ₱42,899 more than today's pair across seven fresh years, with the same drop.
  • Status. As of 29 Oct 2026 the no-target version is in the code but not live yet; the live bot still uses the fixed target.
How to read the pesos. Each year starts again with ₱10,000 on 1 January. Every trade risks 2.5% of the current balance, never more than ₱580 (the old $10 cap), compounding on every trade. Section 6 shows the new rule (risk steps down as the account grows, no cap). Trades are counted in the year they closed.

7. Method and limits

  • Same data, same costs. 4-hour candles on the five coins, Binance prices, exchange fees, slippage and real funding for the five coins. The seven other coins have no funding data (applied equally to all three).
  • A test on past prices. The coins and settings were chosen with hindsight; I-Core's settings were tuned on 2020–2022 and judged on 2023–2026. 2021 (a very strong year) carries a lot of the profit in all three. 2020 and 2026 are part years.
  • The no-target version is not live. It exists in the code and passes its tests; the bot on the server still uses the fixed target until the planned upgrade. Its live record starts on the upgrade date and is judged separately.
  • Trades that were open. A trade open at the end of the data is closed at the last price. Trades are counted in the year they closed.
  • Pairs add trades, not risk limits. Up to five trades per bot can be open at once; the "drop" figures are on closed trades.
  • Not included: the Lucid'io performance fee, withdrawals and deposits, taxes, and any trade the exchange could not fill at the test's price (small accounts have contract-size limits).
