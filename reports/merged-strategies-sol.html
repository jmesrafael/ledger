Merged Strategies on SOL

Research · SOL only · 7 years (2020–2026) · data to 27 Oct 2026

What happens if the current bot is run together with a second, slower trend-following strategy? This report compares three things year by year: the current bot, the trend-follower on its own, and the merged version where both run side by side. "R" is the amount risked on one trade (1R = $5 here), so +10R means ten times the amount risked.

Merged made 2.6× the current bot's profit and was up in all 7 years. The price: it can have two trades open at once, so its worst drop (24.6R) is about double the current bot's (12.8R). Per unit of drop it still came out ahead (11.0 vs 8.3). This is SOL only — see the limits at the end.

How these numbers were made

  • Current bot: the live Lucid’io rules, unchanged (rules are confidential and not described here).
  • Trend-follower: a slow, textbook moving-average strategy. It trades rarely, holds for days and lets winners run until the trend turns. Settings are standard, not tuned to SOL.
  • Merged: both strategies run side by side, each risking its normal 1R. They never block each other, so the merged result is exactly the two added together. Nothing is a new invention.
  • Prices: Binance USDT-M futures, 4-hour candles, 2020 → 27 Oct 2026. Fees, slippage and real funding are included. Every decision uses only candles that had already closed (checked at 2,808 cut-off points: no look-ahead).
  • A year counts when the trade closes. 2026 is a part-year.

7 · Is the comparison fair?

Merged risks up to 2R at once, so it should be compared with the current bot at double size, not at 1R.

Same riskProfitWorst dropProfit ÷ drop
Current bot at double size (2R)+210.8R25.5R8.3
Merged (up to 2R)+271.6R24.6R11.0
Current bot (1R)+105.4R12.8R8.3
Merged at half size (up to 1R)+135.8R12.3R11.0
At the same risk, merged still wins: +271.6R vs +210.8R with a slightly smaller drop (24.6R vs 25.5R). Half-size merged earns +135.8R against +105.4R for the current bot, with a similar drop (12.3R vs 12.8R). The edge is real, but it is about 29% better at equal risk, not 2.6×.

9 · What did not work

Before running them side by side I tried letting the two strategies share a single position (only one trade at a time). They were all worse.

VersionProfitYears upWorst drop
Side by side (the merged strategy above)+271.6R7/724.6R
Single slot, first signal wins+89.0R7/711.5R
Single slot, hold winners to the trend end+210.5R5/713.6R
Current bot alone+105.4R7/712.8R

With one slot, a long trend trade blocks the current bot's better signals. Holding winners to the trend end made more money but lost in two years. Running them side by side is the clean answer.

10 · Limits — read before relying on this

SOL only. The merged strategy has not yet been run on ZEC, NEAR, ADA or GALA at full size. An earlier half-size test across all five coins was also positive (+599R vs +493R, smaller drop) but was not up in every year on every coin, so SOL alone looks better than it may be elsewhere.

The trend-follower is carried by a few huge trades. One could not assume 2021 and 2024 repeat. Judge it on whether you can sit through long flat stretches.

The live bot would need changes. It handles one position per coin; a second strategy needs its own position slot (or a separate account) and clear rules on total risk.
