ZEC is being delisted. Which coin should the three bots (BB-CORE, I-CORE, T-CORE) trade instead, next to SOL, NEAR, ADA and GALA? The answer: ETH

ETH is the best replacement. No coin in the top 100 improves the five-coin portfolio more. It passes every test, it has 10 years of history, the deepest MEXC market ($1.5B a day) and the lowest MEXC fee. Nothing fully replaces ZEC: ZEC's lead comes from one year (2025, +100R). With ETH the portfolio earns about 14% less but is smoother and its risk is spread over all five coins instead of sitting on one.

How it was tested

The same three bots that run on the VPS, unchanged, on every candidate coin.

  • The engine is the bots' own code. The research engine was run against the bots' core files (the same files the VPS runs, checked byte for byte) on ZEC, ETH, BTC, DOGE, XRP and SOL: 16 of 18 bot/coin pairs identical trade for trade; the other 3 trades out of ~6,000 differ only where the price equals the Bollinger middle to the last decimal.
  • Rules: BB-CORE (Bollinger 20,2 close-through + SMA200, stop at the middle band, 48-candle exit), I-CORE (Ichimoku cloud break + SMA200, 2 ATR stop, 7 ATR trail), T-CORE (BB-CORE entry, 2 ATR stop, 14 ATR trail). TRI-CORE = 50/25/25.
  • Costs: 0.05% fee per side, 0.02% slippage per fill, real Binance funding. Every coin also re-run at double and triple costs.
  • Data: Binance spot 4h candles (futures only start in 2019-20); Poloniex for 2016-2019; Binance or MEXC futures for coins without a spot market.
  • Gates fixed before seeing results: 7 full years, all three bots positive, ≥ 6 of 7 years positive, positive before and after 2023, ≥ 4 of 5 market regimes positive, positive at double costs, enough MEXC volume.

Does a high backtest rank predict the future?

Barely. Ranking the 39 seven-year coins on 2019–2022 and checking 2023–2026 gives a rank correlation of only 0.17; ZEC itself was 16th before 2023 and 1st after. So the choice rests on robustness (consistency, drawdown, diversification, liquidity), not on the biggest total.

Excluded: VET (strong but under $0.5M a day on MEXC), XLM (5 of 7 years), ONE (best recent numbers but price $0.0016, thin market, a 2022 bridge hack: itself a delisting risk).

What 10 years add

  • BTC is the best coin on its own: smallest drop, best return ÷ drop, and positive in all 7 market phases (2017 bubble, 2018 bear, 2019, 2020-21 bull, 2022 bear, 2023-24, 2025-26).
  • ETH has the biggest total and is the only coin where T-CORE made money all 10 years; but half its total is 2017, when markets were thin, so that year counts for less.
  • As ZEC's replacement, ETH still beats BTC: portfolio return ÷ drop 19.8 vs 17.4, because ETH's bot results move more independently of the other four coins (correlation 0.26 vs 0.42). Taking both (6 coins) scores 19.4: no better than ETH alone.
  • No coin is positive every year. Only ADA (9 of 9) is, and the bots already trade it.

ETH's weaknesses, and what to do next

Weaknesses

  • BB-CORE alone is weak on ETH since 2023 (+0.18R a trade); most of ETH's recent edge comes from T-CORE.
  • ETH lost in 2023 (−8R) and in the 2023-24 phase; ZEC lost there too (−15R).
  • ETH falls together with BTC in crashes; its bot results still correlate only 0.18–0.26 with the other four coins.
  • Contract size: one MEXC ETH contract is 0.01 ETH (about $20.50), twice ZEC's ($9.74). On T-CORE's ~$40 account a wide stop can need less than one contract: check how the bot rounds before switching.

Backups

  • BTC: the best 10-year coin on its own; second choice.
  • ARB, ENA: tie ETH on short histories; watch, do not trade yet.

What the data does not prove

  • Spot prices, not MEXC futures prices; funding is zero before each coin had a futures market. 2016–2019 markets were thin, so those years count for less.
  • Choosing the best of many coins flatters the winner: past rank predicted future rank only weakly (0.17).
  • MEXC volume is a one-day snapshot (Nov 11, 2026). Results are in R; dollars depend on each bot's size and on contract rounding.
