XRP, BNB, ONE and DOT: where they fit

Four coins came out of the replacement studies: XRP, BNB, ONE and DOT. This report tests each one on each bot, with the bot's own rules and real costs, against the owner's rule (6–7 winning years, few losing months, low drawdowns, small losses and big wins: stability over luck), and shows what each does to the lineup the bot trades today: how much profit it adds and how much risk it adds.

The answer at a glance

Lineup effect, Jan 2021 → Oct 2026: profit added and the change in the worst drop (trade by trade), and the share of losing months before → after. Improves = better return per unit of drawdown, no more losing months, Sharpe not lower. Worsens = clearly lower on both. Otherwise a trade-off: more profit for more risk.

Verdict

  • XRP: Worsens +63R profit, +5.5R worst drop, losing months 37%, rule fails 4/6.
  • DOT: Trade-off +86R profit, +7.7R worst drop, losing months 34%, rule fails 2/6.
  • BNB: Trade-off +83R profit, +8.0R worst drop, losing months 36%, rule fails 5/6.
  • ONE: Trade-off +96R profit, +8.9R worst drop, losing months 33%, rule fails 4/6.

I-CORE (today SOL, NEAR, ADA, GALA: +464R, worst drop 24.1R, 43% losing months)

  • BNB improves it outright. Best of the four by return per unit of drop: BNB (22.4 vs 19.3 today).
  • BNB: Improves +111R profit, +1.6R worst drop, losing months 39%, rule fails 3/6.
  • XRP: Trade-off +127R profit, +6.7R worst drop, losing months 39%, rule fails 3/6.
  • ONE: Trade-off +93R profit, +6.0R worst drop, losing months 43%, rule fails 5/6.
  • DOT: Worsens +36R profit, +13.7R worst drop, losing months 49%, rule fails 6/6.

T-CORE (today SOL, NEAR, ADA, GALA, ETH: +655R, worst drop 34.5R, 46% losing months)

  • ONE improves it outright. Best of the four by return per unit of drop: ONE (21.0 vs 19.0 today).
  • ONE: Improves +283R profit, +10.3R worst drop, losing months 46%, rule fails 2/6.
  • DOT: Trade-off +103R profit, +7.7R worst drop, losing months 47%, rule fails 5/6.
  • BNB: Worsens +145R profit, +16.0R worst drop, losing months 50%, rule fails 3/6.
  • XRP: Worsens +48R profit, +10.9R worst drop, losing months 49%, rule fails 6/6.
Alone vs inside the bot. The owner's rule judges a coin on its own; the lineup test judges it inside the bot, which is what the account lives through. They can disagree. BNB on I-CORE fails the rule alone (it loses in 52% of months) but lowers I-CORE's losing months from 43% to 39% for only +1.6R of extra drop, because it moves on its own (correlation 0.17): its bad months tend to land when the other coins win. For the account, the lineup result is the one that counts.
Stability over luck. A coin that adds profit but also adds drawdown, losing months or a bad year is buying return with risk. The owner's rule asks for the opposite, so a coin should go live only when it is marked Improves (the lineup gets steadier, not just bigger); a Trade-off coin is a paper-test candidate first, and a Worsens coin stays out. ONE should not go live on any bot whatever its numbers: a $0.0016 coin with a bridge-hack history and thin volume could be delisted, the very problem that started this search.

Limits

  • Backtest on Binance candles with real fees, slippage and funding; the engine reproduces the bots' code trade for trade. In-sample: these four were chosen after screening ~120 markets, which flatters them.
  • DOT starts trading in Aug 2020, so its 2020 is a part year. MEXC volume is a one-day snapshot (Nov 11–12, 2026).
