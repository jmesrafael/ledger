The short answer

  • No, 1h is not better in every way. It makes more profit (22% more total R), but its drawdowns are deeper in 3 of the 5 account tests.
  • It is mostly more trades, not a better trade. Average R per trade is almost the same (0.443 and 0.457), and profit factor too (1.71 and 1.72). 1h takes about 3 more trades a month.
  • The lead looks real but smaller on MEXC. +120R on MEXC candles. Before 2020 it is slightly behind (−11R).
  • It would not diversify. Monthly results move together (correlation 0.94), so they win and lose in the same months.
  • It has no live record. Only 4h has been checked against live trades.
Recommendation. Keep 4h live and untouched. Run 1h on paper until the December 4 review, then decide. The question then is whether 27% to 47% more money is worth the deeper drops, such as −58% against −47% from 2023.

3. Is the 1h lead real?

Month by month, 1h beat 4h in 42 of 81 months. The total gap is +130R. Resampling the months 20,000 times gives a 95% range of +37R to +226R, so it is unlikely to be luck on this data. Correlation between the two: 0.94.

4. The paper test and what switching would take

Paper test

  • What runs. A separate copy of the bot, on simulated money from $250, with the live risk steps. It reads MEXC's 1-hour candles and cannot place orders: real trading is disabled in its code and it holds no API keys.
  • Checked. The bot's decision code replayed on MEXC's 1h history gives the same 1,454 entries as the backtest. 6 trades differ in R, all time exits across a hole in MEXC's candles, because the bot counts clock time and the backtest counts candles.
  • What a month can show. At about 20 trades a month, the December 4 review can show whether it behaves as tested. It cannot prove the edge.
  • What paper cannot show. Real fills, MEXC refusals, and funding, which paper mode does not charge.

Before 1h could go live

  • New risk limits. The stop-and-review line of about −26R was set for 4h. 1h's own backtest drawdown (−31.2R) already goes past it. That line and the −39R halt would need to be set again.
  • Code changes. 4h is built into the live bot, including its candle reader, signal window, compare tool and dashboard. Moving the settings alone would stop the bot trading, with no error shown, because it only looks back 400 candles and the trend filter needs 800.
  • The owner's plan. It would restart the 3 to 6 month untouched run decided on November 4.
  • Accounts. The bot refuses to open a coin where the account already holds a position it did not open, on both timeframes. Running 4h and 1h side by side with real money would need a separate MEXC account.

How it was made

  • Rules. Signals use closed candles only and fill at the next candle's open. If a candle touches the stop, the stop counts first. One position at a time per coin; a signal while a coin is in a trade is ignored.
  • Costs. bb-core: fees and 0.02% slippage each way, plus real Binance funding paid or received during each trade. I-Core and the trend strategies: 0.05% fee and 0.02% slippage each way, plus a flat funding estimate.
  • Pesos. ₱63 per $1. Position size is set when the trade opens, from the closed balance. Risk is the live bot's steps (2.5%, 2% from ₱31,500, 1.5% from ₱63,000, by the highest balance so far), with a total cap of ₱3,150 per trade unless stated. Balances count closed trades only, on the day they close.
  • 1h settings. The 4h lengths multiplied by 4: Bollinger band 80, trend filter 800, time exit 192 candles. The ATR filter was left at 14, 0.3; changing it did not change any trade, because the stop distance is always above it.

Limits

  • These are past prices, not a forecast. The 1h version has never traded live.
  • Large peso numbers are compounding on past trades. Exchange minimum sizes and thin order books would change the early and the largest results.
  • Results are not independent proof: the settings were checked on the same market history they are reported on, apart from the checks shown separately.
  • Not included: the Lucid'io fee, taxes, deposits and withdrawals.
