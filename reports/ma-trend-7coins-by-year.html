Which years and which coins? MA trend systems on 7 coins

The five bot coins (SOL, ZEC, NEAR, ADA, GALA) plus BTC and ETH, 4h chart, 2019 to 5 Nov 2026 (2026 included). The ten moving averages are built like the bots: 200 trend filter, 2 ATR stop, 14 ATR trailing stop, long only. Their settings were chosen on BTC, ETH, SOL and ADA only, so ZEC, NEAR and GALA are coins they never saw. The real bb-core and I-Core ran on the same candles. Checked bar by bar in the StrategyBacktester replay: no repaint, no look-ahead.

The short answer

  • Most profitable overall: SMA (+1160R), HMA (+1152R), DEMA (+1140R). The spread between the ten is small; all ten out-earn both bots in total R (+974R or more against +799R and +803R).
  • Best coins: ZEC (+229R), BTC (+220R), SOL (+185R). Weakest: NEAR (+101R) and GALA (+71R), still positive for every indicator. The bots do best on GALA, ADA and ZEC/SOL; the MAs do best on the big, trending coins.
  • Best years: 2021 (+449R) and 2024 (+384R) are about 74% of the total. 2025 is solid (+166R).
  • Bad years: 2022 loses -48R for every one of the ten (the bots made +124R and +51R that year) and 2023 is flat (+17R). 2026 so far: +56R for the MAs, +131R for bb-core and +86R for I-Core.
  • The price: a deeper worst drop than the bots (-72% to -84% at 2.5% risk on one pooled account, against -58% for bb-core and -76% for I-Core) and a win rate of only about 21%.
How the numbers work. R = the amount risked on a trade. Years count trades by the year they closed. "Fresh ₱10,000" tables start each January with ₱10,000 and risk 2.5% of the balance per trade. Seven coins trade at the same time, one position per coin.

4. No repaint, no look-ahead: replayed in the StrategyBacktester

The strategy is now in the app as ★ MA Trend · 4h ATR trail (file strategies/ma-trend-strategy.js, also in the registry and the offline cache). It runs in the same Replay as the other strategies, candle by candle.

1) streaming MAs vs batch: 470580 bar-values, 0 mismatches
2) VWMA price×MA200 replay trades 469, mismatches vs batch 0, max |R diff| 2.5e-10 (ATR seed differs in the 7th digit)
2) ALMA 50×200 replay trades 301, mismatches vs batch 0, max |R diff| 4.6e-10 (ATR seed differs in the 7th digit)
2) HMA price×MA20 both sides replay trades 964, mismatches vs batch 0, max |R diff| 1.7e-6 (ATR seed differs in the 7th digit)
2) KAMA price×MA100 both replay trades 520, mismatches vs batch 0, max |R diff| 2.3e-8 (ATR seed differs in the 7th digit)
REPLAY CHECK: 2254 trades on 7 coins x 4 strategies, 0 mismatches
  • Streaming indicators: the app strategy updates each moving average once per closed bar. All ten match the batch indicators at all 470,580 bar-values tested (0 mismatches), so a value cannot change after its bar closed.
  • Replay engine: a bar-by-bar copy of the app's order rules (decision on a closed bar, fill at the next open, stop touched inside the bar, trailing stop live from the next bar). The strategy is only shown candles 0 to i at each step. Four different strategies on all 7 coins gave 2,254 trades that match the research run in count, entry bar, exit bar and R.
  • Indicators were also recomputed on truncated data in the first report (300 points, 0 differences), and I-Core's own trades are reproduced exactly by the same trade loop.

Run it live in the app

  1. Start the app (npm run serve), pick a coin on 4h, open Strategy and choose ★ MA Trend · 4h ATR trail.
  2. Settings: VWMA, "Price crosses MA", length 200, trend filter 200, stop 2 ATR, trail 14 ATR, risk 2.5%, Long only. Fill at Next bar open, session filter off, commission 0.05%, slippage 0.02%.
  3. Press Replay and watch it trade candle by candle. Funding is not in the app, so expect slightly better numbers than this report.

Honest limits

  • 2022 loses for all ten and 2023 is flat: this is a "wait for the big trend" system; long stretches pay nothing.
  • Long-only is a bet that these coins keep drifting up. The bots trade both sides.
  • Seven coins are far fewer than seven independent bets (BTC, ETH, SOL and ADA move together).
  • Each setting was chosen on 4 coins and 2019-2026 data; ZEC, NEAR and GALA are real out-of-sample coins, and 30 of those 30 results are positive, but the period still contains the 2021 and 2024 rallies.
  • One pooled account drops -72% to -84% at 2.5% risk; the bots -76% and -58%. At 1% risk the drops are roughly half as deep (4-coin check: -39% instead of -71%).
