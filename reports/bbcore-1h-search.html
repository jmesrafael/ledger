The short answer

  • The pick is bb-core's own logic on 1-hour candles. It uses the live rules with lengths multiplied by 4, with nothing tuned to the results. It is profitable in all 7 years, including 2026 (+121R).
  • It holds up. Every nearby setting tried stays profitable in all 7 years. At 10 times the assumed slippage (0.20%) it still makes +571R.
  • The trend strategies look bigger but are not safe. Their ending balances run from ₱2,319,191 to ₱4,668,113, with win rates of only 11% to 15% and drops of −55% to −86% from 2023. Most of their profit comes from 2021, 2024 and 2025.
  • Almost everything shows a paper profit. 680 of 684 variations made money both before and after 2023. These coins trended strongly, so a profit in this test alone proves little. That is why the choice rests on consistency and drawdown.
Status. The live bots still run on 4 hours and nothing has changed for them. The 1h version is being tested on paper. See the report "bb-core 4h vs 1h" for the full comparison and recommendation.

5. How this was made, and the limits

  • Rules. Signals use closed candles only and fill at the next candle's open. If a candle touches the stop, the stop counts first. One position at a time per coin; a signal while a coin is in a trade is ignored.
  • Costs. bb-core: fees and 0.02% slippage each way, plus real Binance funding paid or received during each trade. I-Core and the trend strategies: 0.05% fee and 0.02% slippage each way, plus a flat funding estimate.
  • Pesos. ₱63 per $1. Position size is set when the trade opens, from the closed balance. Risk is the live bot's steps (2.5%, 2% from ₱31,500, 1.5% from ₱63,000, by the highest balance so far), with a total cap of ₱3,150 per trade unless stated. Balances count closed trades only, on the day they close.
  • 1h settings. The 4h lengths multiplied by 4: Bollinger band 80, trend filter 800, time exit 192 candles. The ATR filter was left at 14, 0.3; changing it did not change any trade, because the stop distance is always above it.
  • No look-ahead. Each indicator gives the same values when the history is cut short and re-run, on 3 cut points per coin for BTC and SOL, using the 4h settings of the same code. The bot's own decision code was also replayed bar by bar on MEXC 1h candles: all 1,454 entries match the backtest.
  • I-Core on 1h was not tested. The scaled settings gave 0 trades and that was not solved, so it is not compared here.

Limits

  • These are past prices, not a forecast. The 1h version has never traded live.
  • Large peso numbers are compounding on past trades. Exchange minimum sizes and thin order books would change the early and the largest results.
  • Results are not independent proof: the settings were checked on the same market history they are reported on, apart from the checks shown separately.
  • Not included: the Lucid'io fee, taxes, deposits and withdrawals.
  • The trend strategies use a flat funding estimate, not real funding, so their numbers are less exact than bb-core's.
