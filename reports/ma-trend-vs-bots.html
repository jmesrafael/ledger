The short answer

  • The ten indicators are nearly the same. Once tweaked, the choice of moving average moves the average per trade by only a few tenths of R. What makes money is the recipe, not the formula.
  • The recipe: trade only in the direction of the 200 average, risk a tight 2 ATR stop, and trail it far behind price (10 to 20 ATR) with no profit target. Long-only beat long-and-short by a wide margin from 2023 on and also in the earlier years.
  • Does it beat the bots on profit? Yes. 90% of the 270 settings in the recommended zone beat I-Core's total R from 2023 on, 84% beat bb-core's, and 100% beat I-Core's average per trade. It is not one lucky setting.
  • Does it beat them on smoothness? No. It wins only 20% of its trades (bots 30 to 34%), its worst drop is about the same or a bit deeper, and almost all its profit came in two years (2021 and 2024). bb-core was profitable in every year.
  • Status: research only. Nothing here touches the bots or the live account.
How to read the pesos. Each year starts again with ₱10,000 on 1 January. Every trade risks 2.5% of the current balance, compounding on every trade, no cap. Trades are counted in the year they closed. The coin returns are pooled: all four coins trade at the same time, each one position at a time.

Honest limits

  • Long-only is a bet on crypto drifting up. BTC, ETH, SOL and ADA rose over 2019-2026. A long bear market would hurt it; the bots trade both ways.
  • Four coins are about 1.5 independent bets. They move together, so four positions can lose together.
  • Few trades. The second part has about 110 to 340 trades per setting and 20% of them win; the profit comes from a handful of long trends.
  • Many settings were tried (4,320). Trust the zone median and the share of settings that beat the bots, not the single best row.
  • Funding is a flat 0.005% per 4h bar (I-Core's assumption), not the real history. Liquidity and slippage at size are not tested.
  • No repaint, no look-ahead: indicators are recomputed on truncated data and match (300 points, 0 differences); the trade loop reproduces I-Core's own trades exactly.
Next step worth testing: running the tweaked MA together with bb-core. The bots earn in years the MA system does not (2022, 2023, 2026) and the reverse, so the pair may give a smoother curve than either. Not done in this report.
