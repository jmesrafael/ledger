Risk & Compounding

Reference data · Lucidyo · data to 21 Oct 2026

Three questions answered with backtest data: why October 2026 is red, how deep drawdowns have gone, and what happens when each trade risks 1%–5% of the balance with compounding. "R" is the amount risked on one trade (at $1 risk, 1R = $1).

How these numbers were made

  • Strategy: Lucidyo, our in-house trend-following system. Its rules are confidential and are not described here. Every trade has a fixed stop, so a loss is capped at about 1R.
  • Prices: Binance USDT-M futures, Jan 2021 → 21 Oct 2026. Fees, slippage and real funding are included.
  • Coins: SOL + ZEC (the starting plan) and 4 coins (SOL, ZEC, NEAR, ADA).
  • Compounding: before each trade, risk = balance × risk %. The balance updates when a trade closes. Both coins share one balance.

1 · Why October 2026 is red

SOL + ZEC−6.7R−7.7R14.6R

It's not because the strategy started this month. October is tested with the same rules and the same finished candles as every other month. The rules were fixed on 2019–2022 data, so 2023–2026 was already unseen, and it was profitable.

The cause is the market. ZEC rallied to about $1,640 on Sep 27, then fell to about $1,150 with sharp bounces. Longs near the top were stopped out, and then shorts on the way down were stopped by the bounces: 7 ZEC losses in a row. On Oct 18 one market-wide bounce hit SOL, ZEC and NEAR together. Trend-following systems make money on clean trends and lose in reversals and chop.

  • As a full month, it's within the normal range. SOL+ZEC lost −7.3R in Dec 2025 and −6.6R in Apr 2022, and 20 of 69 months since 2021 were red.
  • As a 3-week stretch it's the worst start to a month on record. It's unlucky, but well inside the risk plan.
  • After the 9 earlier months that lost 3R or more, the next month was positive 8 times (average +4.9R). That's a small sample, not a promise.

4 · Each year restarted at $100, with months shuffled

Each calendar year starts again at $100 on 1 January and compounds within that year. To check that the result isn't just lucky timing, each year's months were shuffled into 2,000 random orders and also reversed.

Every year, at every risk level, in every order, ended above $100, for both SOL + ZEC and the 4 coins. With compounding, the year-end result barely depends on the order of months. What the order changes is how low the balance dips along the way.

5 · Recommended risk

StageSOL + ZEC4 coins
First 2–3 months (proving live matches backtest)1%1%
After that, if live trades match the backtest2%1%
Hard ceiling3%2%
  • 2% on SOL + ZEC is the sweet spot. It earns roughly double the 1% result, the worst drawdown was about 28%, and only 2 of 58 one-year starts lost money.
  • 4–5% isn't worth it. In weak years (2022, 2026) it earned the same as 3% or less, while the balance could dip to $40, or to $17 with 4 coins. A 65% drawdown needs about +190% just to get back to even.
  • Small accounts can't size exactly. At $100 and 1%, real risk per trade varies from about $0.50 to $1.70, because one SOL or ZEC contract step is worth about $11–13. Sizing gets precise as the balance grows.

Limits of this data

  • These are simulations on past prices, not real trades. No live trades had been placed when this was written.
  • The coins were picked partly because they had good backtests (hindsight), so expect future years to look more like 2022 and 2026 than 2021.
  • Shuffling only rearranges each year's real trades. It can't show a year worse than any that has already happened.
  • GALA is not included (no data file). ADA had an open short at −0.58R that isn't counted.

Past results, simulated or real, do not guarantee future returns. Only use money you can afford to lose. Data: Binance USDT-M futures prices and funding to 21 Oct 2026. Generated 22 Oct 2026.
