ETH on T-CORE

T-CORE (bb-core entry, 2 ATR stop, 14 ATR trail) started trading ETH live on 12 Nov 2026, in place of ZEC, which is being delisted. This report shows how ETH has done on T-CORE every year and every month, runs it through the trading-math checks, compares it with the five coins T-CORE traded, and gives a verdict. BB-CORE and I-CORE do not trade ETH: on those two bots it ranks last of six coins.

The answer: ETH is T-CORE's steadiest coin

On T-CORE, ETH made money in every calendar year we have data for: 2017 to 2026 (7 of 7 on the main 2020–2026 data, 10 of 10 on the 10-year series). On the same 2022–2026 window it has the fewest losing months, the shallowest drawdown and no losing year of the six coins. It is not the biggest earner: ZEC and SOL made more R a month, with far bigger swings. Two honest caveats are on page 4: its profit comes from about 10 big trends, and the drawdown it actually had (10.4R) was a lucky order of trades; plan for 15–24R.

The 10-year run (Dec 2016 → Nov 2026) has T-CORE positive on ETH in 10 of 10 years. Ignore the size of 2017: ETH went from about $8 to $1,400 that year on thin Poloniex markets, and a 14 ATR trail with a 2 ATR stop turns a 100× trend into hundreds of R. It shows the rules ride a real trend; it is not a return to expect. 2018–2019 are also thin-market years and count for less.

Reading the years. The pattern is the same every year: about a quarter of the trades win, losers are small (−1R), and a few long trends pay for everything. The weakest year on the 7-year data was 2023 (+1.9R), when ETH chopped sideways; it still finished positive. The best years (2021, 2025) each came from one or two big trends.

What the months show

  • 27 of 82 months lost money (33%), 26 were flat, the rest made money. The longest run of losing months was 3.
  • The typical (median) month made +0.00R; the average month +2.18R. The gap is the trend system at work: most months are small, a few are large.
  • Best month +50.4R, worst month −6.2R. The worst 12 months in a row: −4.9R; 4% of all 12-month periods ended negative.
  • The longest time below a previous high was 12 months.

With ZEC, T-CORE made more (+554R vs +419R) and had the better return ÷ drawdown (15.1 vs 12.2): most of that came from ZEC's 2025 rally, and ZEC carried 58% of T-CORE's risk on its own. ZEC is leaving either way: against the four coins alone, adding ETH adds +92R for 4.0R more drawdown.

Verdict

ETH on T-CORE: a good long-term fit, and the right replacement for ZEC. It has been positive every year we have data for, has the shallowest drawdown and the fewest losing months of the six coins, works whether BTC is rising or falling, and its edge is statistically solid (t-stat 2.46). It will not repeat ZEC's 2025 windfall: expect T-CORE to earn somewhat less than it did with ZEC, with smaller swings.

What to watch

  • Long losing runs are normal: about 27% of trades win; up to 9 losers in a row happened. Judge ETH on months and years, not on single trades.
  • Drawdown to expect: plan for about 15R, 24R in a bad case (1 in 20). The 10.4R in the backtest was a lucky order of trades. A drop past ~30R would be outside the tested range and worth a review.
  • Everything rides on the big trends: without its 10 best trades ETH on T-CORE is about −7R. The bot must stay up and take every signal.
  • Contract size: 1 MEXC contract = 0.01 ETH (≈ $20). At today's ~$100 balance a normal stop sizes to 2–3 contracts, so the real risk can be up to ~15% under target. A stop wider than about 12% skips the trade rather than oversizing it.
  • Not on BB-CORE or I-CORE: ETH ranks last of six coins on both (deep drawdowns, 3–4 years below a high). Keep it on T-CORE only.
