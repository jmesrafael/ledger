What the coins have in common

  • Win rates are low on purpose. BB-CORE wins about 1 trade in 3, I-CORE 1 in 3, T-CORE about 1 in 5. They make money because wins are much bigger than losses. Long losing runs are normal: up to 17 losses in a row on BB-CORE, 16 on I-CORE and 27 on T-CORE happened in the past.
  • ZEC is the biggest earner on I-CORE and T-CORE, mostly from one year. Its 2025 rally gave I-CORE +133R and T-CORE +203R. ZEC lost money in 2023 on all three bots.
  • ADA is the most reliable old coin (all 7 years up on BB-CORE, 6 on I-CORE) but has gone quiet lately: +7R, +2R and −7R over the last 12 months on BB-CORE, I-CORE and T-CORE.
  • NEAR and ARB are hot right now (strong 2026 on every bot) after weak years: NEAR lost 2020 and 2025 on I-CORE and T-CORE (and 2023 on I-CORE); ARB lost 2023 on both bots it trades on.
  • GALA is BB-CORE's best coin by total and by profit per trade, every year up. On I-CORE and T-CORE it lost 2025.
  • ETH is T-CORE's steadiest coin: up all 7 years, a worst drop of only 10R on +177R profit, and its 5 best trades are only 73% of its total (every other T-CORE coin is near or above 100%).

How realistic are these numbers?

What the test includes, what it leaves out, and what to expect live.

  • Same rules as the VPS bots. The research engine was checked against the bots' own core files trade by trade (16 of 18 bot/coin pairs identical; 3 trades out of ~6,000 differ on a rounding tie). BB-CORE: Bollinger close-through + SMA200, stop at the middle band, 48-candle time exit. I-CORE: Ichimoku cloud break + SMA200, 2 ATR stop, 7 ATR trailing stop. T-CORE: BB-CORE entry, 2 ATR stop, 14 ATR trailing stop (rides long trends).
  • Costs included: 0.05% fee per side, 0.02% slippage per fill, real Binance funding payments from each coin's futures listing.
  • Prices: Binance spot 4-hour candles (MEXC futures history is too short and too thin before 2023). MEXC prices track Binance closely, but a live fill can be a little worse than a test fill on a fast candle.
  • Window: trades closed from Jan 1, 2020 to Nov 11, 2026. Each coin counts only from when it had 200 candles of history (the SMA200 filter needs them).
Expect live results to be lower than this. The bot settings were chosen by looking at this same history, so the test has seen the answers. Earlier research (ZEC replacement, Nov 11) found that how a coin ranked in 2019–2022 barely predicted how it ranked in 2023–2026 (rank correlation 0.17). Treat the totals as the upper end, and the year-by-year pattern (most years up, some flat, long losing runs) as what to plan around.

What this means for running the bots

  • Judge each bot over a year, not a month. Even in this test 34% of BB-CORE's months, 45% of I-CORE's and 48% of T-CORE's were not up.
  • T-CORE is the most profitable and the bumpiest. Worst drop 50R vs 31R for BB-CORE and 33R for I-CORE. Almost all its profit comes from a few long trends (2021, 2024, 2025); in a year without them (2023) it goes sideways.
  • No coin needs removing on this data. Every coin on every bot made money over its full history. The weakest spots are ARB on BB-CORE (thin profit, two losing years), NEAR on I-CORE (three losing years) and SUI on T-CORE (short history). ADA has gone flat lately but has the longest good record.
  • Most of the I-CORE and T-CORE gain in the last 12 months is ZEC (+184R and +245R). Without ZEC their last 12 months would be +46R and +111R: still up, but far smaller.
