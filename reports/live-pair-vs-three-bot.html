The answer

Your live pair is already a good, honest edge: it made money in all 7 years and in 70% of months, and it earns in rising, sideways and falling markets. Nothing tested replaces it. The one change that helps is adding a third bot, T-CORE, with a quarter of the money, taken half from I-CORE. It catches long trends that the other two exit early, and it moves almost independently of BB-CORE (correlation -0.08).

Why the mix is more flexible. BB-CORE makes small, steady gains and is strongest in sharp falls. I-CORE rides medium trends and is the best in sideways months. T-CORE holds a trade for weeks while a big move lasts. Each covers a market the others handle badly, so the bad months of one are often the good months of another. The mix's worst month was −9.6R against −10.6R for the live pair.
What it costs. A third bot means a third account, more positions and more margin. In 2022 and 2023 it made less than the live pair (₱14,154 and ₱13,347 against ₱16,169 and ₱20,179), and it was picked after looking at the data. Paper-test it first.

The mix was found after looking at the results. These tests check whether the idea still works when the choice is made blind.

  • Of 55 possible third-strategy sleeves added at 30% to BB-CORE, 54 beat BB-CORE alone after 2023 and 32 beat the live pair. Adding a different kind of trade is the edge, not one lucky indicator.
  • The TRI-CORE mix scores 20.5 before 2023 and 20.5 after, the steadiest of all 11. The live pair goes 15.7 → 14.7.
  • Monte Carlo: in 5,000 reshuffled 7-year paths, none lost money overall. The bad-luck 5% still ended above +431R for the mix and +433R for the live pair.

What to do

1
Keep both live bots

No change on the VPS. BB-CORE and I-CORE stay as they are.

2
Paper-test T-CORE

Same entry as BB-CORE, 2 ATR stop, 14 ATR trailing stop, no time limit. Run it on paper next to the live bots until the 4 December review.

3
Then move a quarter

If the paper results look like this test, move 25% of the money into it, taken from I-CORE's share.

How this was tested

  • SOL, ZEC, NEAR, ADA and GALA, Binance USDT-M 4-hour candles, real funding, 0.05% fee per side and 0.02% slippage per fill. Entry at the next candle's open, stop checked first in every candle.
  • The engine reproduces the real BB-CORE trade for trade (594.2R both). No look-ahead (5,040 checks, 0 mismatches).
  • Each bot is its own account, sized with the live risk steps at ₱60 per $1. Drops are measured on closed trades.
  • Kept all 7 years with compounding, the mix would end near ₱14.9M and the live pair near ₱14.8M. That is not realistic: exchange size limits, leverage caps and thin markets on ZEC and GALA would stop it long before. The yearly figures are the useful ones.
  • Not modelled: margin and leverage limits, the halt rule, and funding after 21–26 Oct 2026. The coins mostly rose from 2020 to 2025, and live trading usually does worse than a backtest.

What the data does not prove

  • The return gap is small. Total profit is +631R (live), +661R (TRI-CORE) and +655R (TRI-CORE 1h). Without 2024 the live pair and TRI-CORE earn the same (549R vs 545R), because one month, January 2024, gave TRI-CORE +45R against +11R.
  • Both mixes were picked after looking at the results. Neither third bot has run live yet; the T-CORE lab and the 1h paper bot were built on 6 Nov and, per the project notes, were not yet installed on the VPS.
  • TRI-CORE 1h has a practical blocker as well: the 1h backtest drawdown of −31.2R is past the −26R review line, the live bot has 4h hard-coded, and it would need its own MEXC account.
  • Not in the tests: median trade, PCA of the bot streams (2.5), VaR (4.1), margin limits and the halt rule.
Verdict. Judged on the trading-math tests, TRI-CORE is the better mix: it is the only one that improves diversification, drawdown, tail risk and out-of-sample stability together. TRI-CORE 1h earns more pesos a year but buys it with the same bet twice and a deeper drop. Keep paper-testing both until the 4 December review; do not size the 1h mix as if it were three independent bots.
