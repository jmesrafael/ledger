The risks, and what to watch

Picked after seeing the results. The three additions were chosen from the same 42 months they are scored on. Expect less than the backtest shows. The coins go live without a paper period, on the owner's decision of Nov 12, 2026.
A few big months carry the profit. 60% of SUI's T-CORE profit came in one month (Feb 2024), about half of ARB's T-CORE profit in one month (Oct 2026), and 72% of ARB's BB-CORE profit came in 2026. ARB lost money on BB-CORE in both 2023 and 2024.
The coins move together. SUI, ARB and the current coins are all altcoins and can fall at the same time. The account's worst drop did not get deeper in the test, but a market-wide sell-off hits every coin at once.

How it goes live

1
Dashboard database

Allow SUI and ARB in the bot event log (one prepared SQL change). Without it the dashboard rejects the new coins' trades.

2
BB-CORE

Update the bot on the VPS, then switch ARB on in its settings. 1 MEXC contract = 1 ARB (about $0.15), so sizing is fine-grained.

3
T-CORE

Install the new live build (coins SOL, NEAR, ADA, GALA, ETH, SUI, ARB). 1 contract = 1 SUI (about $0.81). Open trades and the account are kept.

Backtest on Binance 4h prices with real fees, slippage and funding; MEXC volume on Nov 12, 2026: SUI about $37M a day, ARB about $15M a day (GALA, already traded, about $1M).
