A Fifth Coin for BB-CORE and I-CORE

ZEC is gone from all three bots. T-CORE took ETH in its place, but on BB-CORE and I-CORE ETH ranks last, so those two bots now trade four coins. This report asks whether any other market can fill the fifth slot: about 120 markets, including MEXC's stock, index, gold and oil contracts, each tested on each bot with the bots' exact rules and real costs.

The answer: no new coin clears the bar, two come close

The owner's rule for adding a coin is stability over luck: on that bot, the new coin must be at least as steady as the middle of the four coins the bot already trades, on every measure (years won, losing months, worst drop, worst year, size of wins against losses). No coin passes all of them, so BB-CORE and I-CORE stay on four coins.

Two coins come close and are worth knowing about:

  • DOT on BB-CORE wins more months than any coin the bot trades (59% against 56–58%) and has a smaller worst drop than SOL or ADA. It misses on win size (wins 2.39× its losses; the bot's coins about 2.96×) and on its worst year (−0.6R). Added to the bot, profit rises about 19% (+460R → +546R) but the bot's worst drop rises about 28% (27.4R → 35.1R).
  • BNB on I-CORE wins fewer months (41%) than SOL, ADA or GALA (about 50%), so it fails the rule, but it is as good as NEAR, which the bot already trades (40% of months, 4 of 7 years). It has the best win size of all (5.35×). Added to the bot, losing months fall (43% → 39%), profit rises (+464R → +575R) and the worst drop barely moves (24.1R → 25.7R).
Recommendation. Keep BB-CORE and I-CORE on four coins. If the owners want a fifth coin anyway, BNB on I-CORE is the stronger case (it makes the bot smoother as a whole); DOT on BB-CORE buys more profit with a deeper drop. Either one should run in paper mode first.
Correction to an earlier draft. An earlier version of this study recommended DOT and BNB because it measured drawdown only at month-end, which hides dips that recover inside a month and made both coins look like they lower the drop. Measured trade by trade, as here, they raise it. The profit totals and years won were the same in both versions.

Stocks, gold and oil on MEXC: none fit

MEXC also lists about 30 liquid non-crypto contracts (gold, silver, oil, S&P 500, Nasdaq, NVDA, SNDK, MU, TSLA, SK Hynix and more). They were tested on up to 13 years of prices (Dukascopy and Yahoo, stock splits corrected), on the 24/7 candles MEXC shows (quiet nights and weekends). Every market with a long history fails: gold made about +0.1R a year with a 69R drop, the S&P 500 and Nasdaq about +1R a year or less, and big tech mostly lost money on BB-CORE. The few that look good (SNDK, SK Hynix, MSTR) have under 3 years of data, all from the 2025–26 chip rally, and their results swing wildly with how closed hours are handled. These bots are built for crypto trends.
